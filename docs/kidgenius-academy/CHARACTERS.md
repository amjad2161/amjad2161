## Supporting Characters

### Leo (ليو)

A small, adorable lion cub wearing a red adventure vest. Brave and helpful,
he guides children through the nature worlds.

### Hadar (הדר)

A cartoonish clementine creature with a permanent smile. Wears an apron
printed with fraction symbols (½, ¼). Teaches mathematics at the Citrus Market.

### Oz (عوز)

An ancient ethereal girl spirit, 300 years old. Floats centimeters above the
ground in a white linen dress embroidered with golden academy blueprints.

### The Silver Owl (البومة الفضية)

An elegant silver-white owl representing the calm inner guide. Feathers glow
under moonlight.

### The Black Owl (البومة السوداء)

Cosmo's sibling and story antagonist. A black owl with sad features and
disheveled broken feathers expressing pain and loneliness — not evil. Its
harshness fades when puzzles are solved.

### The Desert Child (طفل الصحراء)

A Bedouin boy with sharp bronze features, golden eyes, and sandstorm marks on
his cheek. A mysterious, competitive character in adventure worlds.

### Maia (مايا)

The academy's digital guardian. Short chestnut hair interwoven with golden
circuit traces. Wears a full white protection suit.

### Amal (أمل)

A friendly Arab child in a classic blue jacket and a red-gold keffiyeh scarf.
Wears a small oud-shaped necklace and serves as the linguistic and musical
bridge.

## Design Specifications for 3D Modeling

All character meshes follow these constraints:

- **Topology:** Quad-dominant, subdivision-ready
- **Proportions:** Oversized heads (roughly 1:3 head-to-body ratio)
- **Materials:** Soft subsurface scattering on skin, matte fabrics
- **Rigging:** IK foot placement for walk cycles across uneven terrain
- **Blendshapes:** Expressive facial morph targets (happy, sad, surprised, thinking)
- **Reduced-motion support:** Idle bob animations can be disabled per `prefers-reduced-motion`
