# KidGenius Academy — Vehicles & Transition Mechanics

The project does not use cars or fuel-powered vehicles. Instead, it relies on
**magical and physics-based transport** that fits the fantasy world:

---

## 1. Floating Platforms (الجزر الطائرة)

Rocky earth chunks covered in green grass and light particles. They function as
carriers and carts, transporting characters between the academy's gate portals.

**Physics:** Gentle vertical bob animation with soft ease curves.

---

## 2. Keyhole Portals (بوابات العبور الفضائية)

3D keyhole-shaped gates that emit dynamic colored light and hover with a gentle
vertical motion. The camera pushes through the portal for seamless world
transitions without loading screens.

**Transition:** Camera compression through the keyhole opening with a brief
radial blur effect.

---

## 3. Pixel's Roller Wheels (العجلة المغناطيسية لبيكسل)

The magnetic wheels beneath the Pixel robot, equipped with orange-rimmed tires,
enable precise sliding movement across the programming grid.

**Physics:** Magnetic snap-to-grid with momentum-based slide.

---

## 4. Cosmo's Flight Guide (جسر الطيران الفضائي)

Long-distance travel is handled by flying behind Cosmo the owl. The
surrounding world pulls back with high-efficiency motion blur, simulating rapid
gliding through the educational sky.

**Effect:** Directional motion blur with depth-of-field narrowing during
flight sequences.
