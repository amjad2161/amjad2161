# KidGenius Academy — Environments & 3D Worlds

The academy contains **10 primary worlds** distributed as visible regions
floating in the academy's educational space.

---

## 1. Academy Hub (ساحة الأكاديمية)

A floating island above cotton-like clouds. A crystalline dome sits at its
center, flanked by white stone walkways with golden edges. Holographic lanterns
hover in the air, and a rotating spherical hologram projects the interactive
world map.

**Lighting:** Soft angled sunlight at 4500 K.

---

## 2. Citrus Market (سوق الحمضيات)

A covered market shaded by cheerful orange and yellow umbrellas. Round wooden
tables hold gleaming fruits, and brass balance scales sit at every stall.

**Key assets:** `citrus-market-environment`, `citrus-fruit-set` (orange, lemon,
lime GLB models).

---

## 3. Logic Lab (مختبر المنطق)

A futuristic lab with pure-white walls and blue-green neon accents. Conveyor
belts carry cubes, holographic screens display interactive programming
challenges, and small succulents decorate the shelves.

**Key mechanic:** Grid-based block coding for the Pixel robot.

---

## 4. Anatomy World (عالم الأناتومي)

A giant holographic human-body model that children walk through. Translucent
pink walls reveal blood vessels and heartbeat rhythms — designed to be
educational without being frightening for young users.

**Key assets:** `friendly-body-explorer-gltf`.

---

## 5. Science Garden (حديقة العلوم)

An enormous glass greenhouse filled with glowing flowers and plants growing
inside chemistry beakers. States of matter (water, ice) are simulated in
real-time.

**Key mechanic:** Drag clouds for rain and sunlight for growth.

---

## 6. Animals Kingdom (مملكة الحيوان)

A multi-climate environment combining tropical rainforest, polar ice, and wild
farmland. Children classify animals by habitat and solve food-chain puzzles.

**Key mechanic:** Drag-and-drop animal classification into biomes.

---

## 7. Music Stage (منصة الموسيقى)

An open-air stage shaped like a pearlescent shell with purple-gold glow.
Musical notes decorate the floor, and oversized percussion instruments (lemon
piano, watermelon drums) line the stage.

**Key mechanic:** Rhythm repetition within 5 ms latency.

---

## 8. Feelings World (عالم المشاعر)

A soft cloud space whose colors shift dynamically to reflect emotional states:
pink for love/empathy, violet for calm, blue for sadness. Color transitions
complete in 1.2 seconds.

**Key mechanic:** Empathy choice triggers warm golden lighting.

---

## 9. Language Universe (يوم اللغات)

A cosmic library where books orbit in space. Arabic, Hebrew, and English
letters float as glowing asteroids, assembling into words when connected
correctly.

**Key mechanic:** Constellation-style letter tracing in three languages.

---

## 10. Academy Finale (אולם הגמר)

A grand graduation hall with stained-glass star ceilings and walls draped in
achievement banners and golden feathers. The three golden locks guard the final
gate.

**Key mechanic:** Combined puzzle finale unlocking three locks.

---
